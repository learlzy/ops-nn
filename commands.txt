--------------------------mod_unsqueeze_cast--------------------------------------
编译：
bash build.sh --pkg --experimental --soc=ascend910b --vendor_name=custom --ops=mod_unsqueeze_cast

安装：
./build_out/cann-ops-nn-custom-linux.aarch64.run

设置环境变量：
export LD_LIBRARY_PATH=/home/developer/Ascend/cann-9.0.0/opp/vendors/custom_nn/op_api/lib/:${LD_LIBRARY_PATH} 

运行(aclnn)：
bash build.sh --run_example mod_unsqueeze_cast eager cust \
--experimental --example_name=mod_unsqueeze_cast --vendor_name=custom

运行(graph):
bash build.sh --run_example mod_unsqueeze_cast graph cust \
--experimental --example_name=mod_unsqueeze_cast --vendor_name=custom

卸载：
bash ${ASCEND_HOME_PATH}/opp/vendors/custom_nn/scripts/uninstall.sh

debug专用环境变量：
export ASCEND_SLOG_PRINT_TO_STDOUT=3
export ASCEND_GLOBAL_LOG_LEVEL=0
